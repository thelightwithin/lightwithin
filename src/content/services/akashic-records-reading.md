An Akashic Records reading is a guided session that connects you to an energetic record of your soul's experiences, past and present, often referred to as the "Book of Life". It offers insight into patterns, relationships, and life themes so you can better understand what is happening in your life and why.

Each session expands your awareness and access to energetic upgrades aligned with your clear intentions.

You will be given a format for formulating questions, suggestions for preparation, and a review of the logistics of the Akashic Record Reading ahead of time (and again at the onset of the session). Your guide will clear the energy for optimal connection ahead of time and, when possible, provide you with a recording of the session or guide you on what you may record.

Many people use these readings to gain clarity, release blocks, and move forward with a stronger sense of direction and alignment.
