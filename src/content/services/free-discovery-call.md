Not sure where to begin? The discovery call is a soft landing. We talk about where you are, what is calling you toward this work, and which offering fits — or whether now is simply not the time. Both are welcome answers.

There is nothing to prepare and nothing to commit to. Come as you are.

**Good for:** first-timers, the curious, anyone choosing between services.
